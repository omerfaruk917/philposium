How to submit

The Department of Philosophy at Marmara University invites undergraduate students to present and discuss their research at Philposium 27'. The symposium welcomes work in all areas of philosophy, with no set theme. Interdisciplinary work is welcome when it poses a clear philosophical question, advances a philosophical argument or offers critical analysis.

Who can apply?

Undergraduate students in Türkiye and abroad are eligible to apply. The symposium will be held in Turkish and English.

Abstract requirements

  • Write 300–500 words in Turkish or English.
  • Use a title that reflects the content and clearly state your research question, main thesis and principal arguments.
  • For blind review, remove your name and any other identifying information from the attached file.
  • Only an abstract is required at the application stage.

Application and contact

Include the paper title, 3–5 keywords, your full name, university, department, year of study and email address in your message. Attach the anonymised abstract as a file.

Send submissions and questions to this address.

philposium@marmara.edu.tr

Review

The Scientific Committee receives only anonymised abstracts. It assesses philosophical content, clarity and coherence of argument, originality and academic competence.

Presentation

Each paper is allotted 15 minutes for presentation, followed by 10 minutes for questions and discussion.

Publication opportunity

At the authors’ discretion, full versions of papers presented at the symposium may be considered for publication in Ufuk Felsefe Dergisi, a journal published by philosophy students at Marmara University.
